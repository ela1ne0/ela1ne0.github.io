Designing playful visuals for Duke's premier hackathon

HackDuke 2026 ✴ Lead Landing Page Designer ✴ 2025–2026

Context

HackDuke is a student-run hackathon at Duke University that aims to enable all students to use technology for social good. For the 2026 event, I was individually tasked with the end-to-end design of the main landing page.

Following the launch of the landing page, I collaborated within a 7-member design team to develop a cohesive web ecosystem and custom assets for the extended scroll sections, ensuring a unified brand identity across the entire digital experience.

Application & Site Flow

  • The Hook (Landing Page)

    As the sole designer for the initial landing experience, I established the visual anchor—a character-driven approach featuring a dragon mascot. This served as the foundational "hero" moment that set the tone for the rest of the site.

  • Collaborative Scaling (Scroll Sections)

    After defining the core aesthetic, I worked alongside the 7-member team to translate that visual language into modular scroll sections, ensuring every touchpoint felt like part of the same "atmospheric" universe.

Results

~1,000 Registrations (Up 100% YoY)
80% Increase in engagement
10+ Unified brand touchpoints

Hello Applicant!

  • Multitaskers: High-contrast CTAs for quick registration.
  • Atmosphere-seekers: Drawn to playful, unique visual systems.
  • Mobile-first: Optimized for social media referrals.

Reflection

"This project pushed me to design beyond individual screens. I learned how to balance expressive branding with strict usability constraints and how to collaborate across design and development without losing visual cohesion."

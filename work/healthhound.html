Health Hound

Humans constantly release chemical signals through breath and skin, but we have no way of perceiving them. These signals can indicate changes in metabolism, illness, or inflammation long before symptoms appear. The challenge was to design a speculative tool that could detect an invisible aspect of human sensory experience and translate it into something users can understand and act on.

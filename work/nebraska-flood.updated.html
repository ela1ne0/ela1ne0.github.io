Nebraska Flood 2019

Visualizing the impact of the historic 2019 Nebraska floods through an interactive data-driven experience. I led the design of the mapping interface and narrative structure to help users understand the scale of the disaster.

RoleLead UI/UX Designer
Team3 Designers
Timeline3 Months
ToolsFigma, Mapbox, React

The Goal

The objective was to create a visceral, easy-to-navigate platform that combined real-time water level data with personal stories from affected communities.

Design Approach

We prioritized "Apple-like" restraint and polish, using high-contrast data visualizations and clean spacing to ensure the heavy subject matter remained readable and impactful.

Deliverables & Contribution

Deliverables

  • Interactive flood map UI
  • Narrative data visualization system
  • Mobile-responsive site architecture

What I Contributed

  • COLLABORATIVE: Narrative flow & data vetting

Flood Risk Advocacy

In collaboration with the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, I designed a multi-channel communication system to translate complex GIS flood-risk data into accessible, human-centered visuals for low-income rural populations. By bridging the gap between deep learning analytics and user-facing design, I helped create a framework adopted by U.S. HUD-related stakeholders to assist in disaster recovery patterns.

RoleProduct Design & Graphic Design
TeamJust me!
Timeline3 Months
ToolsFigma, Mapbox, React

The Challenge

flood data is often presented as high-density GIS maps that are unintuitive for the average resident, particularly in communities with lower digital literacy. The challenge was to transform "scary" technical data into actionable, inclusive design that provides clear paths to safety and recovery.

Design Approach

We prioritized "Apple-like" restraint and polish, using high-contrast data visualizations and clean spacing to ensure the heavy subject matter remained readable and impactful.

HackDuke 2026

HackDuke is a student-run hackathon at Duke University that aims to enable all students to use technology for social good. For the 2026 event, I was individually tasked with the end-to-end design of the main landing page, collaborating with a 6-member design team to scale this identity.

    The Challenge

    Every year, the HackDuke design team selects a unique visual theme for the event. The challenge is to completely overhaul the digital presence to match this theme while improving usability and conversion metrics. I inherited the 2024-25 "Underwater Rave" theme (deep purples/neons) and was tasked with leading the transition to the 2025-26 "Urban Convenience Store" theme.

    The Strategy & Action

    • Humanizing the Tech Experience: While the previous "Space" theme was high-contrast and modern, it felt "cold" and abstract. The "Convenience Store" theme utilized warm, sunset tones and familiar urban imagery to create a sense of community and "accessibility," making the hackathon feel like a welcoming neighborhood hub rather than an exclusive tech event.
    • Reduced Cognitive Load: The "Convenience Store" concept provided a clear, real-world metaphor for information architecture. By treating sections of the site like a physical storefront (e.g., the "Apply Now" poster on the window), I reduced the mental friction for new users, leading to a more intuitive navigation flow.
      • Visual Narrative & Engagement: The illustrative, lo-fi aesthetic encouraged users to linger on the page to find "easter eggs" (like the cat in the storefront), increasing time-on-page and successfully driving the 80% increase in registrations. Strategic Contrast: By moving away from the standard "dark mode/neon" hacker trope, the 2026 landing page stood out in a saturated market of student tech events, strengthening HackDuke's online visibility by 50%.

    Reflection

    This project pushed me to design beyond individual screens. I learned how to balance expressive branding with strict usability constraints and how to collaborate across design and development without losing visual cohesion.

HackDuke 2026

HackDuke is a student-run hackathon at Duke University that aims to enable all students to use technology for social good. For the 2026 event, I was individually tasked with the end-to-end design of the main landing page, collaborating with a 5-member design team to scale this identity.

The Challenge

Every year, the HackDuke design team selects a unique visual theme for the event. The challenge is to completely overhaul the digital presence to match this theme while improving usability and conversion metrics. I inherited the 2024-25 "Underwater Rave" theme (deep purples/neons) and was tasked with leading the transition to the 2025-26 "Urban Convenience Store" theme.

Strategy & Execution

While I worked within a 5-member team for general site assets, the landing page was my solo project. I wanted to move away from "standard tech" (think: darker colors, sleek, minimalist design...) and lean into a lo-fi, "anime-esque," character-driven narrative.

  • Thematic Pivot: Transitioned the visual language from a dark, ethereal space aesthetic to a warm, "lo-fi" urban sunset palette, using a 2D illustrative style to make the event feel more communal and approachable.
  • Solo Execution: While collaborating with a 5-member team on general assets, I was the sole designer responsible for the 2026 landing page's layout, asset creation, and user flow.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Utilized Figma Make to quickly wireframe the new "Storefront" concept, reducing early design iteration time by 30%.
  • Front-End Integration: I worked closely with the HackDuke tech team to implement the site in React, ensuring the final build matched the Figma designs 1:1 — including custom illustrations and responsive behavior.

Social Media Campaign

In addition to the website, I also designed a series of countdown and announcement graphics for Instagram to maintain visual consistency across platforms. The goal was to extend the “Convenience Store” theme into a high-impact social format optimized for engagement.

  • Modular Countdown System. I designed flexible Figma templates to support daily countdown updates without rebuilding assets from scratch.
  • Brand Cohesion. The gradients, illustrations, and urban motifs carried seamlessly from the landing page into social posts, creating a consistent experience across platforms.

The Impact

  • +80% Increase in Registrations: Registrations increased from ~500 to ~900 participants following the new site launch and thematic shift.
  • Brand Consistency: Developed 20+ digital graphics/assets that translated the "Convenience Store" theme across all social channels, boosting online visibility by 50%.

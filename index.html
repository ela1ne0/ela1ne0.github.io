About

TL;DR

I’m a product and visual designer at Duke University studying Computer Science and Visual Media Studies. I’m passionate about building complex, narrative-driven digital experiences with a high bar for polish and a focus on social impact.

MY DESIGN STRENGTHS

I enjoy building things end-to-end — from early sketches and prototypes to polished interfaces and working code. I lean into LLMs as tools, allowing me to focus more on storytelling and thoughtful interaction.

System-level thinking: I move beyond individual screens to build modular libraries. I’m passionate about creating reusable component systems that allow teams to scale effectively while maintaining a consistent visual voice across web and physical environments.

GOAL

I want to design tools and experiences that feel intuitive, playful, and carefully crafted.

I FILL MY FREE TIME WITH...

Catching up with friends, trying new restaurants, making more design, taking pictures with my digital camera, and curating Pinterest boards and Spotify playlists.
